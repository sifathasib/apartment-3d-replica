# 🏡 3D Apartment Digital Twin & Interactive Virtual Tour

A real-time, interactive 3D digital replica and virtual tour of an apartment built from real-world video footage using **Three.js**, procedural architectural shaders, dynamic ambient lighting, and interactive walkthrough controls.

## 📐 Replicated Architectural Features & Rooms

Based on the actual walk-through video footage:

### 1. Central Hallway & Dining Suite
* **Polished White Marble Tiles**: Procedurally generated subtle grey marble veining with realistic surface specular reflection.
* **Dining Suite**: Marble-topped dining table with 4 upholstered modern chairs.
* **Modern Display Credenza**: Sideboard cabinet with glass upper display doors.
* **End Hallway Vanity**: Wall-mounted ceramic washbasin, pedestal, and vanity mirror.

### 2. Elevated Lounge / Study Room (Left)
* **Raised Wooden Platform**: Rich warm oak parquet flooring stepped up from the hallway.
* **Under-Step Glowing Amber LED Strip**: Recessed cove light underneath the step lip that casts a warm golden glow onto the marble floor (toggleable via UI!).
* **White Folding French Doors**: Multi-pane white glass folding partition doors.
* **Furniture**: Dark green leather lounge sofa, window bench seating, wall-mounted AC unit, and overhead spinning fan.

### 3. Main Living Room (Right)
* **Deep Navy-Blue Sectional Sofa**: Signature L-shaped modern sectional sofa with cream and navy accent cushions.
* **Floor-to-Ceiling Curtains**: Pleated sheer window drapery.
* **Modern Coffee Table & Recessed Ceiling Spotlights**.

### 4. Kitchen & Service Area
* Ceramic wall tile borders and dark granite countertop surfaces.

## 🎮 Interactive Controls

* **Dollhouse 3D Mode**:
  * **Left-click + drag** to orbit around the apartment.
  * **Right-click + drag** to pan.
  * **Scroll wheel** to zoom in and out.
* **Cinematic Guided Tour**:
  * Click **"Cinematic Tour"** to sit back and watch an automated drone camera glide between every room in the apartment.
* **First-Person Walkthrough Mode**:
  * Click **"Walkthrough Mode"** to explore at eye level.
  * Use **`W`, `A`, `S`, `D`** (or Arrow keys) to walk forward/back/left/right and drag the mouse to look around.
* **Lighting Deck**:
  * **"LED Step: ON/OFF"**: Toggles the amber cove lighting under the elevated lounge floor.
  * **"Ceiling Fans"**: Toggles fan rotor rotation on or off.
  * **Day / Night Mode**: Switches between natural sunlit day and warm cozy evening ambient lighting.
* **Room Jump Buttons**: Instantly jump the camera to the *Elevated Lounge*, *Main Living Room*, *Dining Hall*, *Kitchen*, or *Dollhouse View*.
